## What we process

### Email you send us

If you write to [kontakt@weekslot.com](mailto:kontakt@weekslot.com), we process the content of the message and the contact details you provide, in order to reply and, where relevant, to add or correct a listing.

### Technical server logs

The site is hosted by Vercel. Vercel keeps ordinary technical server logs, which typically may include IP address, time, the page requested and technical information about the browser. The logs are used for operation, troubleshooting and security.

### No login and no storage form

You do not need an account. There is no form on WeekSlot that stores data with us. Organisers and others who want to tip us off do so by email.

### Ads and cookies (Google)

WeekSlot may show ads from Google AdSense. Google is then a third party that may store or read information in your browser (cookies and similar technologies) in order to show ads, measure them, limit how often an ad is shown, and combat fraud and abuse.

We distinguish between:

- **Non-personalised ads**, which do not build a profile of you in order to tailor the content.
- **Personalised (targeted) ads**, which may use information about activity on this and other sites to show more relevant ads.

Non-personalised ads may also use cookies, for example for frequency capping and aggregated reporting. Without consent to store information on your device, we do not show ads.

Which cookies are set, why, and for how long, is shown in the consent choices and in [Google's cookie overview](https://policies.google.com/technologies/cookies) and [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). See also [how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

We do not use a separate analytics tool such as Google Analytics on WeekSlot.

## Consent and how to withdraw it

Non-essential cookies, including advertising cookies, are not set until you have given consent. You can accept, refuse, or choose purposes separately. It should be as easy to say no as to say yes.

You may withdraw consent or change your choices at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing before the withdrawal. Use the **Consent settings** link in the footer of the site (and here: [Consent settings](#samtykkeinnstillinger)). This reopens the consent choices.

If you refuse, we show no ads. The reserved ad slots on the page stay empty, so the layout does not jump.

## Legal basis

Processing is carried out under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Norwegian Electronic Communications Act (ekomloven), because the controller is established in Norway.

- **Consent** (GDPR Article 6(1)(a) and Article 7) is the basis for advertising cookies and for personal data processed in that context, including personalised ads when you have consented to them.
- **Ekomloven section 3-15** requires valid consent meeting the GDPR standard before anyone stores or accesses information on your communications equipment, except for storage that is strictly necessary to provide a service you have requested, or that is used solely to transmit communication over a network. Remembering your consent choice is such necessary storage. The earlier rule in the 2003 ekomloven section 2-7b was replaced by section 3-15 on 1 January 2025.
- **Legitimate interests** (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)) is the basis for ordinary technical server logs, for operation and security.
- **A contract / a request you initiate** (GDPR Article 6(1)(b), or legitimate interests) is the basis for processing email you send us, so that we can reply and, where relevant, add or correct a listing.

According to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet), other legal bases can almost never be used for the further processing of personal data collected through cookies.

## Retention

- **Email** is kept only for as long as needed to reply and, where relevant, to add or correct the listing, and is deleted when that purpose is fulfilled, unless we are required to keep it longer.
- **Server logs** are kept for the period the hosting provider (Vercel) retains such logs for operation and security.
- **Your consent choice** is stored by the consent solution (Google's Consent Management Platform) for as long as needed to respect your choice, and until you change or withdraw it.
- **Advertising cookies** last for the periods Google states in its documentation and in the consent choices.

## Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, where the conditions in the GDPR are met. You also have the right to withdraw consent, as described above.

To exercise your rights, write to [kontakt@weekslot.com](mailto:kontakt@weekslot.com). We will reply without undue delay, and at the latest within one month.

## Complaints

If you believe we do not process personal data in line with the rules, you may lodge a complaint with Datatilsynet in Norway, the supervisory authority for the controller. We hope you will contact us first, so that we can try to resolve the matter.

Datatilsynet: [How to complain](https://www.datatilsynet.no/om-datatilsynet/kontakt-oss/klage-til-datatilsynet/)

If you are in another EEA country, you may also complain to the supervisory authority in your country of residence. In Sweden that is the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY): [Complaints to IMY](https://www.imy.se/klagomal). In the United Kingdom you may contact the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

## Children's privacy

WeekSlot is aimed at parents and carers planning holiday weeks, not at children. We do not ask for information about children, and we do not want personal data about anyone under 13. Under section 5 of the Norwegian Personal Data Act, children aged 13 or over may in some cases consent themselves to information society services; under 13, the holder of parental responsibility must consent.

If we learn that we have received personal data about a child under 13 without valid consent, we will delete it.

Ads on WeekSlot must not be designed to target children. The default is that personalised ads are not shown until you have, if you so choose, consented to them.
