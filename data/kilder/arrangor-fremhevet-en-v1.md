Get seen by parents planning the holiday week

## Intro
WeekSlot shows week-long courses and camps for kids, sorted by week and municipality. Listing is free.

## What you get
- Your activity appears on the pages for the right week, municipality and type of activity.
- Parents see age, times, price and a link to your sign-up.
- Sign-up and payment stay with you, as before.

## How to get started
1. Send us your activity at kontakt@weekslot.com: name, week, place, age, times, price and a link to sign-up.
2. We add it and let you know when it's live.

Button: Send us your activity
Link: mailto:kontakt@weekslot.com?subject=New%20listing%20on%20WeekSlot

## Questions and answers
**Does it cost anything?**
No. Listing is free.

**Does WeekSlot take sign-ups or payments?**
No. Parents go straight to your own sign-up.

**What if I want to change the details?**
Email us at kontakt@weekslot.com and we'll update it.

## Rules
- No prices, packages or paid placements on the page.
- No promises of views, sign-ups or a "guaranteed place".
- Never write "all camps", "complete overview", "cheapest" or "free forever".
