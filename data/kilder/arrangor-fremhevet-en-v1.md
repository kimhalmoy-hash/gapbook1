## Intro
WeekSlot lists week-long courses and camps for kids, sorted by week and municipality. Getting listed is free. If you want more visibility, you can choose Featured.

## Basic listing – free
- Your activity appears on the pages for the right week, municipality and type of activity.
- Parents see age, times, price and a link to your sign-up.
- Sign-up and payment stay with you, as before.

## Featured – NOK 290 per municipality per week
- Your activity shows at the top of the page for the municipality and week you choose.
- It is clearly marked «Featured», so parents know the placement is paid.
- You choose which municipalities and weeks you want to be featured in.

Example: Featured in one municipality during winter break week costs NOK 290. Two municipalities in the same week cost NOK 580.

## More visibility in the winter, Easter and autumn breaks
- **Top of category – NOK 690 per break:** Your activity shows at the top of one type of activity in one municipality for the whole break, for example «Climbing in Trondheim, winter break». Marked «Featured».
- **Season package – NOK 990 per break:** Featured in your municipality and the neighbouring ones, Top of category, an overview of how many people click through to you, and a «Featured on WeekSlot» badge you can use yourself.

The summer holiday has its own package prices. Ask us at kontakt@weekslot.com.

## How to get started
1. Send us your activity at kontakt@weekslot.com.
2. Tell us which municipalities and weeks you want to be featured in, if you'd like that.
3. We add it and let you know when it's live.

## Questions and answers
**Do I have to pay to be listed?**
No. The basic listing is free. Featured is an option for those who want more visibility.

**Does WeekSlot take sign-ups or payments?**
No. Parents go on to your own sign-up page.

**How do parents know something is featured?**
Every paid placement is marked «Featured».

**What if I want to change my details?**
Email us at kontakt@weekslot.com and we'll update it.

## Rules
- No promises about views, sign-ups or a «guaranteed place».
- Never use «all camps», «complete overview» or «cheapest».
